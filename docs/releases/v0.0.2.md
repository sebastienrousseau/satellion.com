## Highlights ⭐️

* **The whole family on the site**: the company page and passmcp's page list every component of the passmcp family with its release status, and the ones considered and rejected with the reason, rendered from passmcp's `ecosystem.json`.
* **Released, not planned**: passmcp's page shows discovery, passmcp-graph and the compliance mappings as released in 0.0.1, and the roadmap section is gone.
* **Two more Go module paths**: `satellion.com/passmcp-lsp` and `satellion.com/passmcp-census` resolve to their repositories.
