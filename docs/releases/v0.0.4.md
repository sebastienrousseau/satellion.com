## Highlights ⭐️

* **A new design**: every page is laid out as a grid of solid cards that reflows from wide screens to one column on a phone, under a frosted header that turns opaque when you ask your system for less transparency or more contrast.
* **Rated 10/10, and kept there**: the site passes all ten of the generator's quality pillars with no accessibility issues and scores 100 in every Lighthouse category, and the build now fails if any of that slips.
* **Just the family**: the family table lists the nine components and their releases; what was decided against stays in passmcp's documentation.
* **A demo in the README**, captured from the built site by `make demo`.
