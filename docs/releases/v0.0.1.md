## Highlights ⭐️

* **Satellion's home and passmcp's pages**: satellion.com carries the company site, passmcp's product page, its manual and a real sample report, built from passmcp's 0.0.1 release.
* **Go module paths**: `satellion.com/passmcp` and its sibling modules, including the agentgateway processor's nested module, resolve from pages checked on every build.
* **Format URIs**: the attestation and graph formats are published at stable addresses under `satellion.com/attestation/` and `satellion.com/graph/v1`.
